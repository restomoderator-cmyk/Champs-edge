## Open questions for the English version

1. "Head" for голова reads fine in hockey talk ("keep your head"), but "cool head" and "hot head" carry a slight insult in English that the Russian does not. A native reader should confirm.
2. "Core" for стержень loses the image of a rod or spine. Alternatives: backbone, anchor.
3. Period versus shift for отрезок. A real period is twenty minutes; "shift" may fit a 35-second stretch better.
4. "Derby" is a soccer word in North America. "Rivalry game" may be the right term there.
